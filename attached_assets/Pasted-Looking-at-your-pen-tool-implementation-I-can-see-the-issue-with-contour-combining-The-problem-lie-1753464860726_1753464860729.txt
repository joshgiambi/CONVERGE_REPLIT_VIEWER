## Understanding the Geometric Challenge

The reason proper contour merging is complex relates to computational geometry. When you draw a pen stroke that should combine with an existing contour, you're essentially performing a polygon union operation. This requires:

**Intersection Detection:** Determining where the new stroke overlaps with existing geometry
**Polygon Union:** Combining overlapping areas into a single, non-self-intersecting polygon
**Topology Preservation:** Maintaining the medical accuracy of anatomical boundaries

Simply concatenating point arrays creates invalid polygons with self-intersections and disconnected regions, which is why your pen tool contours appear separate rather than combined.
